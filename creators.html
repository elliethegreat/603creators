Now recruiting · Paid program

Engage and educate your state. We'll help.

603 Creator Collective is a paid incubator that helps New Hampshire creators build an independent platform and share high-quality local news and commentary.

Apply
Who should apply

You might be a great fit if…

  • You live in New Hampshire and know your community well.
  • You're unabashedly progressive and pro-democracy.
  • You create on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, or Substack, or want to start.
  • You're curious about the State House and how politics shape daily life.
  • You want to get the facts right, even when it's slower.
What you get

Skills, support, and a network behind you.

Training

Content creation, production, interviewing, fact-checking, and distribution.

Stipend and equipment

A stipend for your time and the gear you need to produce high-quality content.

Briefings

Journalists, policy experts, and elected officials help you decode what's happening in Concord.

Access

Help getting press passes and booking interviews with local leaders.

Community

A statewide network to workshop ideas and get feedback.

Amplification

National creators who share your content and help you grow.
